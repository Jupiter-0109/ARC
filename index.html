Android / OS Hard Blocking Setup:

A Progressive Web App runs inside a browser sandbox and cannot block or quit native OS apps. For true OS-enforced app lockouts:

  1. Open your phone's Settings app.
  2. Tap Digital Wellbeing & parental controls.
  3. Tap App timers $\rightarrow$ set strict daily limits on distracting apps (Instagram, YouTube, etc.).
  4. Tap Focus mode $\rightarrow$ configure work schedules to pause and hide distracting apps while ARC runs.
Use ARC's Focus Shield for mindfulness, session leave accountability, and self-reported budgets, and Digital Wellbeing for hard OS restrictions.
